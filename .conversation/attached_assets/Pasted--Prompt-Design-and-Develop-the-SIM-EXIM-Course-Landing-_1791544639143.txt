# Prompt: Design and Develop the SIM EXIM Course Landing Page

Create a complete, modern, responsive website landing page for **Skills Institute of Management (SIM)** to promote its practical Import–Export (EXIM) Training Course and generate student enquiries.

Use the attached **SIM EXIM Course Landing Page Design Draft PDF** as the primary reference for the content, page structure, section hierarchy, and form fields. Follow the design direction closely while making the website polished, visually engaging, professional, and conversion-focused.

## 1. Brand and Visual Direction

Design a premium education and professional training website.

- Primary colour: dark navy blue `#102A43`
- Secondary colour: professional blue `#1769AA`
- Background: white and very light blue `#F5F9FD`
- Accent colour: warm gold `#F5B544`
- Typography: Inter, Manrope, or a similar modern sans-serif font
- Use generous whitespace, strong headings, clean cards, subtle shadows, thin borders, and consistent spacing.
- Avoid excessive gradients, unnecessary animations, cluttered layouts, and generic AI-generated design patterns.
- The website should feel trustworthy, practical, and business-oriented rather than like a generic coaching institute template.

- SIM logo or a simple text-based logo placeholder
- Home

- EXIM Course

- Contact

Add a prominent blue button: **Enquire Now**

On mobile, convert the navigation into a working hamburger menu.

## 3. Hero Section

Use a two-column desktop layout.

Left column:

Eyebrow:
**PRACTICAL IMPORT–EXPORT & EXIM TRAINING**

Main headline:
**Learn Global Trade. Build Your EXIM Business Foundation.**

Supporting paragraph:
“A structured, practical course to understand the export–import process—from choosing a product and researching markets to buyer verification, documentation, shipping, costing and building a 90-day action plan.”

Primary CTA: **Enquire About the Course**

Secondary CTA: **Explore Course Curriculum**

Add a short supporting line:
“Learn the key stages of an import–export transaction through structured modules and a practical trade simulation.”

Right column:

Create a premium visual representing international trade, using a combination of shipping containers, a cargo ship, a world map, trade routes, logistics, and business documentation. Use authentic-looking photography or polished custom visuals. Avoid cartoon-style illustrations.

Ensure the hero is balanced, with strong text hierarchy and clear calls to action.

## 4. Large Statistics and Numbers Section

Directly below the hero, create a horizontal row of three or four prominent statistic cards.

Use these verified course-structure figures:

- **10** — Core Learning Modules
- **90 Days** — Action Plan Framework
- **Practical** — EXIM Business Simulation

Add a fourth statistic only after the institute confirms a factual claim, such as verified trainer experience or placement support.

Important:
- Do not invent student counts, placement percentages, salary figures, success rates, or years of experience.
- Do not display “100% Placement” unless SIM confirms that the claim is accurate and supported by evidence.
- If verified later, make the placement statistic visually prominent without presenting it as a guarantee unless the institute can substantiate that guarantee.

Use large numbers, concise labels, and a clean white or pale-blue background.

## 5. Course Enquiry Form

Create a prominent course enquiry form immediately after the statistics section. On desktop, use a two-column layout: course information on the left and the enquiry form on the right. On mobile, stack the form below the introduction.

Form heading:
**Enquire About the EXIM Course**

Supporting text:
“Share your details and preferred counselling slot. Our team can follow up with course information.”

Required fields:

1. Full Name
2. Phone Number / WhatsApp Number
3. Email Address
4. Course Selection — default option: EXIM / Import–Export Course
5. Preferred Counselling Date — calendar date picker
6. Preferred Time — dropdown containing:
   - Morning
   - Afternoon
   - Evening
   - No Preference

Add a checkbox for consent to be contacted about course information.

Submit button:
**Request Course Details**

Requirements:
- Validate required fields and email/phone formats.
- Prevent users from selecting past dates.
- Show clear validation messages.
- Display a success message only after the form has been successfully submitted to the configured backend.
- Do not falsely display a successful submission if the form is not connected.
- Do not describe the preferred date and time as a confirmed appointment.
- The date picker must work on desktop and mobile.

If no backend is available, implement the front-end form and clearly document where email, CRM, WhatsApp, or database integration is required.

## 7. Course Curriculum — 10 Modules

Create a professional curriculum section with numbered cards or an accordion layout.

Display all ten modules:

**01. EXIM Fundamentals**  
Understand export, import, trade terminology, and the overall transaction flow.

**02. Business Models and Trade Opportunities**  
Explore trading, sourcing, and different EXIM business approaches.

**03. Business Setup and Registrations**  
Review the setup steps and key registrations relevant to an EXIM business.

**04. Product Selection, HS Codes, and Market Research**  
Assess product ideas, market demand, HS classification, and initial market research.

**05. Finding and Verifying Buyers and Suppliers**  
Learn research methods and basic checks before engaging a trade partner.

**06. International Payments and Risk**  
Understand international payment methods and common transaction risks.

**07. EXIM Documentation**  
Learn the purpose of key documents used throughout a trade transaction.

**08. Logistics, Shipping, and Customs**  
Understand freight, shipment flow, and customs-related processes.

**09. Incoterms, Costing, and Profitability**  
Review trade terms, landed costs, quotation structure, and margin calculations.

**10. Product Compliance and Business Planning**  
Identify compliance questions and organise next steps into a practical plan.

Each module card should include a number, title, short description, and appropriate icon. Keep the layout easy to scan.

Do not invent lesson durations, module hours, or additional certifications.

## 14. Functional and Technical Requirements

Build the landing page as a production-minded, responsive website.

- Use reusable components and clean code.
- Ensure responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Use semantic HTML and accessible form labels.
- Include visible keyboard focus states.
- Ensure sufficient text/background contrast.
- Add smooth scrolling for internal CTAs.
- Make the mobile navigation, curriculum accordion, FAQ accordion, and date picker functional.
- Optimise images and avoid unnecessarily large assets.
- Include basic SEO title, meta description, and heading hierarchy.
- Avoid layout shifts and horizontal scrolling on mobile.
- Do not invent live appointment availability.
- Do not claim that a form submission succeeded without a working submission endpoint.
- If building with React, use clean reusable components and a sensible file structure.
- If using plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, keep code modular and well organised.
